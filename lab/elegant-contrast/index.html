Elegant card: text contrast

Each variant is the real card with its live scene. Under each, two numbers from npm run contrast, measured on the real page at desktop and phone sizes, at a few moments (the northern lights move):

  • Text contrast: the worst line of text against what is behind it, as a WCAG ratio. Small text needs 4.5:1, large text 3:1.
  • Scene kept: how much of the scene's detail still shows through the layer, compared with no layer at all. Higher means the windmills and lights are easier to see.
